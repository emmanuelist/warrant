Warrant

Options that can't default and need no oracle: the collateral is locked inside NEAR Intents until expiry.

An options market on NEAR mainnet. A writer locks collateral inside intents.near and receives options, each a NEP-245 token. A holder can exercise until expiry, paying the strike and receiving the underlying inside Intents. After expiry the writer takes back what wasn't exercised. No price feed is consulted. The contract warrant-options.near has no access keys, so nobody can change its code.
